About This Design

This livery was designed for an Assetto Corsa x Euro Truck Simulator 2 championship, where drivers transport their race car between events on this specially branded trailer before competing in Formula 4 races.

The design draws inspiration from the iconic Jaguar XJR12, the legendary race-winning car that dominated the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1990, bringing its timeless elegance and racing heritage to the trailer's aesthetic.
